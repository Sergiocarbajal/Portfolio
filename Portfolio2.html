Project Poster

This video game called Collided Chaos was my capstone project at Illinois State University. This was the biggest project that I have creacted so far in my game design career. I had the privilege of collaborating with 6 other indivduals all specializing in different areas. The area that I focused on was the coding, specifically the 3D portion of our game. Where I was in charge of coding interactions between the player and enemies, actions for puzzles such as a grappling system as well as object mobility, teleportation between levels, health and respawn mechanics,blockage requiring enemy kills to progress,and the abliity to swap between held items.
